## 1. Platform Overview

**NOVA** is a competition-grade interactive digital prototype of a future city. It is designed to demonstrate **Symbiotic Urbanism**—the principle that artificial intelligence should augment human decision-making, shoulder systemic complexity, and preserve human empathy and democratic oversight.

### Key Architectural Pillars
- **Interconnected Systems**: Eliminating municipal silos so that transit, power grids, healthcare, and ecology self-balance continuously.
- **Human-in-the-Loop AI**: AI models multi-agent complexity and recommends solutions; human citizens and elected councils retain final veto authority.
- **Biosphere Metabolism**: Closed-loop circularity where water, energy, carbon, and materials are continuously replenished.

### ⚡ Section 03 — Shape the Future (Executive Crisis Simulation)
*“The future is a decision. Technology can predict possibilities. Humans decide what matters.”*

The flagship interactive feature of NOVA. You take command of the city across four realistic crisis scenarios.

#### The 4 Scenarios:
1. **Scenario 01: Extreme Heatwave Alert** (+5°C spike, cooling demand surging +42%).
   - *Option A*: Activate District Cooling & Civic Sanctuaries.
   - *Option B*: Surge Renewable Storage Distribution.
   - *Option C*: Mandate Non-Essential Industrial Curtailment.
   - *Option D*: Autonomous Holistic Equilibrium Optimization.
2. **Scenario 02: Arterial Gridlock & Transit Crisis** (Autonomous shuttle collision on Sector 2).
   - *Option A*: Reroute Autonomous AV Fleet to Peripheral Rings.
   - *Option B*: Surge Rapid Transit & Subterranean Maglev.
   - *Option C*: Dynamic Signal & Lane Reversal Synchronization.
   - *Option D*: Absolute Priority Emergency Corridor Lock.
3. **Scenario 03: Peak Energy Deficit & Cloud Interruption** (-38% solar yield deficit).
   - *Option A*: Rapid Discharge of Solid-State Substation Reserves.
   - *Option B*: Prioritize Hospital & Life-Safety Circuits.
   - *Option C*: Activate Peer-to-Peer Neighborhood Microgrid Barter.
   - *Option D*: Automated Commercial Load Shedding.
4. **Scenario 04: Healthcare Surge & Clinical Capacity** (Allergen spike, ED at 94% capacity).
   - *Option A*: Deploy AI Remote Triage & Home Monitoring Kits.
   - *Option B*: Dispatch Mobile Modular Care Units to Community Centers.
   - *Option C*: Surge Medical Volunteer Corps & Mutual Aid.
   - *Option D*: Dynamic Cross-Hospital Algorithmic Bed Balancing.

#### How It Works:
- Every choice uses **deterministic mathematical scoring** (no random numbers).
- Each selection updates your 5-metric city vector: **Sustainability**, **Safety**, **Efficiency**, **Citizen Wellbeing**, and **Innovation**.
- Visual delta badges (`+14%`, `-8%`, `0%`) display the quantified impact alongside analytical narrative feedback.
- Decisions **immediately propagate** down to the **Live City Dashboard** and your **City Profile**.

### ⚖️ Section 04 — Human + AI (“Who Decides the Future?”)
*“Better decisions, not fewer humans.”*

A visual synthesis contrasting computational capabilities with human moral sovereignty.

#### Interactive Ethical Dilemma:
- **Case**: *Incident #882: Winter Cold Wave & Residential Power Curtailment*.
- **The AI Recommendation**: Throttling residential heating by 4°C across 240,000 homes to safeguard regional grid stability.
- **Your Choice**:
  - **ACCEPT**: Enacts the raw algorithmic model. Demonstrates high grid efficiency but exposes vulnerable citizens to cold stress.
  - **MODIFY**: Opens live interactive sliders allowing you to set:
    - *Minimum Residential Warmth Floor* (e.g., 21°C)
    - *Vulnerable Population Exemption Buffer* (e.g., 95%)
    - *Commercial Load Absorption* (e.g., 70%)
    - Shows how human fine-tuning achieves grid resilience without sacrificing vulnerable lives.
  - **REJECT**: Executes an executive council veto, prioritizing human warmth and calling for voluntary community conservation.

### 📊 Section 05 — Live City Dashboard
Real-time monitoring of NOVA's 8 core vital indicators streaming from 1,280 sensor nodes:

1. **Population**: Live tick counter reflecting natural demographic micro-fluctuations (~2,450,120 residents).
2. **Energy Efficiency**: Percentage efficiency of the smart distribution lattice.
3. **Air Quality Index (AQI)**: Micro-particulate PM2.5 index (optimal = 14).
4. **Traffic Congestion**: Congestion percentage across major arterials.
5. **Healthcare EMS**: Average pre-hospital emergency response time in minutes.
6. **Renewable Mix**: Percentage of clean power generated by solar, wind, and geothermal.
7. **Citizen Wellbeing**: Holistic civic satisfaction and health index.
8. **Sustainability Score**: Closed-loop ecological balance score.

> **Dynamic Reaction**: When you change choices in **Section 03 (Simulation)**, all 8 dashboard dials, progress meters, and the Chart.js 24-hour telemetry trend line update in real time.

## 4. Keyboard Shortcuts & Accessibility

- **`Tab` / `Shift + Tab`**: Navigate through all interactive buttons, cards, and sliders with visible high-contrast focus rings (`outline: 2px solid var(--cyan-core)`).
- **`Enter` / `Space`**: Activate buttons, open zones, toggle scenario options, and change timeline eras.
- **`Esc`**: Dismiss the mobile navigation drawer.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion`**: Fully supported; users with vestibular motion sensitivity experience instant opacity transitions instead of multi-axis camera dives and particle flows.
- **Color Contrast**: All typography adheres to WCAG AAA standards (>10:1 contrast ratio against the deep charcoal background).
