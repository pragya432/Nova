## 🏙️ What is NOVA?

NOVA is a competition-grade interactive digital prototype of a future city built around the theme **“Design the Digital Future”**. It departs from generic cyberpunk or smart-city dashboard tropes, presenting a cinematic, living digital twin where computational intelligence augments human decision-making rather than replacing it.

### Core Architecture & Features

1. **City-First Intelligence (New Flagship Feature)**:
   - *City Selection Protocol*: User enters NOVA by specifying their city context (*“Where should we build the future?”*) via real-time autocomplete search, quick-select popular hub pills, or HTML5 Geolocation matching.
   - *5 Supported Launch Cities*:
     - **Dehradun**: Himalayan foothill basin, cloudburst drainage, forest migration corridors, mountain aquifer depletion.
     - **Delhi NCR**: Indo-Gangetic megacity, winter atmospheric inversion, Yamuna river rejuvenation, last-mile feeder mesh.
     - **Mumbai**: Peninsular maritime archipelago, coastal tidal storm surge, suburban rail peak crush, mangrove preservation.
     - **Bengaluru**: Elevated Deccan plateau, cascading lake overflows, Outer Ring Road gridlock, deep borewell groundwater stress.
     - **Hyderabad**: Granitic Deccan hub, Musi river flash flood catchment, HITEC corridor data center grid spikes, pharma-zone effluent control.
   - *Localized City Intelligence Profile*: 8 live dimensional indicators (Environment, Air Quality, Mobility, Urban Growth, Energy, Healthcare, Key Environmental Risk, Canopy Cover).
   - *Analyzed Local Challenges*: 4 localized friction domains per city with direct links to simulation resolution.
   - *Zero-Reload Switching*: Seamless city switching anytime via navbar and Hero HUD badges with persistent `localStorage` memory.
2. **Procedural 3D Digital Twin (Hero)**: Real-time procedural urban model rendered with Three.js featuring 1,400 moving particle traces along autonomous transit and energy corridors, atmospheric fog, and live telemetry HUD overlays with dynamic city coordinates.
3. **Interactive Spatial Map (Section 01)**: 2.5D architectural sector map featuring 6 core domains:
   - *Transport*: V2X mesh orchestrator with route mode toggles.
   - *Energy*: Bidirectional pipeline visualizer with an interactive peak load balancer slider.
   - *Healthcare*: Automated 5-step emergency scenario walkthrough.
   - *Education*: Adaptive AI lifelong learning matrix vs. legacy schooling.
   - *Environment*: Closed-loop circular metabolism telemetry.
   - *Governance*: Human-in-the-loop civic oversight platform.
4. **Connected Intelligence (Section 02)**: Real-time network topology canvas connecting the central AI Core through all 6 sectors to Citizen Wellbeing, accompanied by an interactive 6-stage emergency incident cascade simulator.
5. **Shape the Future Simulation (Section 03)**: Deterministic multi-scenario executive simulator adapting dynamically to the selected city's 4 realistic crises (e.g. Doon Valley Flash Floods vs. Delhi Winter Inversion vs. Mumbai Tidal Surge vs. Bengaluru Lake Cascade vs. Hyderabad Musi Runoff), calculating exact shifts across Sustainability, Safety, Efficiency, Citizen Wellbeing, and Innovation.
6. **Human + AI Decision Matrix (Section 04)**: Visual synthesis of AI capabilities vs. human ethical sovereignty, including an interactive policy dilemma with **ACCEPT**, **MODIFY** (featuring real-time sliders), and **REJECT** pathways.
7. **Live City Dashboard (Section 05)**: 8 vital indicators and synchronized 24-hour Chart.js telemetry trend lines that dynamically react to the chosen city's baseline and simulation decisions.
8. **Future Timeline (Section 06)**: Interactive milestone scrubber spanning 2026, 2030, 2035, 2040, and 2050.
9. **Your NOVA Profile (Section 07)**: Dynamic 5-dimension radar chart, city archetype generator (*The Symbiotic Architect*, *The Human-First Pioneer*, *The Resilient Arbiter*, *The Algorithmic Vanguard*), reset button, and clipboard export.
10. **Synthetic Audio Synthesizer**: Pure Web Audio API ambient soundscape and micro-sounds (muted by default with a toggle).
11. **Research Grounding**: Credible sources table categorizing real-world research (MIT Senseable City Lab, Stanford HAI, IEEE Smart Cities, UN Habitat, IPCC, WHO) vs. NOVA future concepts.
