## 🏙️ What is NOVA?

NOVA is a competition-grade interactive digital prototype of a future city built around the theme **“Design the Digital Future”**. It departs from generic cyberpunk or smart-city dashboard tropes, presenting a cinematic, living digital twin where computational intelligence augments human decision-making rather than replacing it.

### Core Architecture & Features

1. **Procedural 3D Digital Twin (Hero)**: Real-time procedural urban model rendered with Three.js featuring 1,400 moving particle traces along autonomous transit and energy corridors, atmospheric fog, and live telemetry HUD overlays.
2. **Interactive Spatial Map (Section 01)**: 2.5D architectural sector map featuring 6 core domains:
   - *Transport*: V2X mesh orchestrator with route mode toggles.
   - *Energy*: Bidirectional pipeline visualizer with an interactive peak load balancer slider.
   - *Healthcare*: Automated 5-step emergency scenario walkthrough.
   - *Education*: Adaptive AI lifelong learning matrix vs. legacy schooling.
   - *Environment*: Closed-loop circular metabolism telemetry (AQI 14, greywater recycling, canopy cooling).
   - *Governance*: Human-in-the-loop civic oversight platform.
3. **Connected Intelligence (Section 02)**: Real-time network topology canvas connecting the central AI Core through all 6 sectors to Citizen Wellbeing, accompanied by an interactive 6-stage emergency incident cascade simulator.
4. **Shape the Future Simulation (Section 03)**: Deterministic multi-scenario executive simulator (Heatwave, Traffic Crisis, Energy Deficit, Healthcare Surge) calculating exact shifts across Sustainability, Safety, Efficiency, Citizen Wellbeing, and Innovation.
5. **Human + AI Decision Matrix (Section 04)**: Visual synthesis of AI capabilities vs. human ethical sovereignty, including an interactive policy dilemma with **ACCEPT**, **MODIFY** (featuring real-time sliders), and **REJECT** pathways.
6. **Live City Dashboard (Section 05)**: 8 vital indicators and synchronized 24-hour Chart.js telemetry trend lines that dynamically react to simulation decisions.
7. **Future Timeline (Section 06)**: Interactive milestone scrubber spanning 2026, 2030, 2035, 2040, and 2050.
8. **Your NOVA Profile (Section 07)**: Dynamic 5-dimension radar chart, city archetype generator (*The Symbiotic Architect*, *The Human-First Pioneer*, *The Resilient Arbiter*, *The Algorithmic Vanguard*), reset button, and clipboard export.
9. **Synthetic Audio Synthesizer**: Pure Web Audio API ambient soundscape and micro-sounds (muted by default with a toggle).
10. **Research Grounding**: Credible sources table categorizing real-world research (MIT Senseable City Lab, Stanford HAI, IEEE Smart Cities, UN Habitat, IPCC, WHO) vs. NOVA future concepts.
